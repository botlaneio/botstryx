## The problem

Half of scheduling pain is time zone maths. "10:30 your time" goes wrong the moment one side guesses, and it goes wrong twice a year around daylight-saving changes.

## How it works

- Your availability is set in your time zone.
- Each guest sees slots in their own time zone, detected automatically, with an easy way to change it.
- The conversion is DST-safe. A slot that crosses a daylight-saving switch still lands at the right moment for both sides.

## A worked example

You are in Kolkata (UTC+5:30) and offer Mondays 14:00–17:00. A guest in New York (UTC-4 in October) sees those hours as 04:30–07:30 their time. Nobody does the maths, and nobody books a 2 a.m. call by mistake.

## What it does not do

It does not show the guest your time zone by default. They always see their own.
