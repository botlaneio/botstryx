Available times are shown in the guest's timezone. You set your availability in your own timezone.

## How it works

- Your availability is set in your time zone.
- Each guest sees slots in their own time zone, detected automatically, with an easy way to change it.
- The conversion is DST-safe. A slot that crosses a daylight-saving switch still lands at the right moment for both sides.

## A worked example

You are in Kolkata (UTC+5:30) and offer Mondays 14:00–17:00. A guest in New York (UTC-4 in October) sees those hours as 04:30–07:30 their time.
