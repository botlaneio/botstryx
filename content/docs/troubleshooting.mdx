Symptoms are written the way you would type them into search. Each one gives the likely cause and the fix.

## I connected Google Calendar but bookings are not appearing

Likely cause: the calendars slotLane checks for busy times are not the one it writes bookings to, or the Google connection was revoked.

Fix: reconnect Google Calendar and confirm which calendar receives bookings.

## A guest says they never got the confirmation email

Likely cause: on Self-host, confirmations go over your own SMTP, so check those settings first. On any plan, the email may be in spam.

Fix: ask the guest to check spam, then review your SMTP configuration. The booking itself is safe. It lands in your dashboard either way.

## Two guests booked the same slot

This should not be possible. The final check runs inside a database transaction with a unique index, and one person gets a "just taken" message. If you see a true double booking, treat it as a bug and [contact us](/contact).
