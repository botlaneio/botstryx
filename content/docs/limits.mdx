The free plan is for one person: unlimited bookings, calendar sync and video links.

Try everything free for 30 days, no card needed. After that, nothing is deleted. You can stay on the free plan for one person, or subscribe at $10 a month for each person who can be booked ($8 billed yearly).

## What a subscription adds

Subscribing brings back your team, round robin, collective events, routing forms and paid bookings.

Anyone guests can book counts as a person, you included. People you deactivate do not count, and you can change the number of seats at any time.

## Google Calendar

slotLane checks Google Calendar for conflicts and adds every booking to it. A Google Meet or Zoom link can go in the invite.
