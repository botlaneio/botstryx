Set up your booking page in a few minutes. 30 days free, no card needed. slotLane is at [slotlane.cloud](https://slotlane.cloud).

## Set your hours

Weekly hours with several ranges a day, days off and your timezone. Add buffers, minimum notice and how far ahead people can book.

## Create a meeting type

A title, a length, a location and any questions you want answered. Google Meet, Zoom, a link or in person.

## Share the link

Guests see times in their own timezone, pick one, and get a confirmation with a calendar invite.

See also [Booking page](/docs/guides/booking-page), [Google Calendar](/docs/guides/google-calendar) and [Limits](/docs/limits).
