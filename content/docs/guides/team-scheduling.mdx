Add the people guests can book, each with their own hours, calendar and sign-in. Round robin shares bookings fairly across the team. Collective events need everyone free at once.

The free plan is for one person. Subscribing brings back your team, round robin, collective events, routing forms and paid bookings.

Anyone guests can book counts as a person, you included. People you deactivate do not count, and you can change the number of seats at any time.
