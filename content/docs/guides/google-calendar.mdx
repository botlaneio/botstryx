Google Calendar is the calendar slotLane syncs with. The connection checks your busy times, writes each booking, and updates or removes the event when plans change.

## Before you start

- The Google account that owns the calendar you take meetings on.
- If you use Zoom, your Zoom account details for that integration.

## Steps

1. Connect your Google account and grant the permissions slotLane asks for.
2. Choose the calendars slotLane checks for busy times. Include every calendar that can make you unavailable.
3. Choose the calendar slotLane writes bookings to.
4. On each event type, pick Google Meet or Zoom. The meeting link is created for each booking.

## Good to know

- Calendar tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
- Disconnecting a calendar stops sync. Existing bookings stay on the books.

## Common mistakes

- Not checking a second calendar. Personal appointments block your time only if that calendar is in the busy check.
- Writing to a shared calendar you do not control. Bookings are updated and removed as plans change. Pick a calendar you own.
