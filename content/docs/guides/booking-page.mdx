Your booking page shows the times you are free. Guests see those times in their own timezone.

## Hours

Weekly hours, with several ranges a day. Days off. Your timezone. Buffers, minimum notice, and how far ahead people can book.

## Meeting types

A title, a length, a location and any questions you want answered. Google Meet, Zoom, a link or in person.

See also [Google Calendar](/docs/guides/google-calendar), [Time zones](/docs/how-it-works/time-zones) and [Custom domain](/docs/guides/custom-domain).
