Your booking page can live on your own domain, for example book.yourcompany.com, on both Cloud and Self-host, with no vendor branding.

## How it works per plan

- Cloud: the custom-domain setup is done for you. You point the domain. The certificate and hosting are handled.
- Self-host: the included Caddy setup fetches and renews the HTTPS certificate once your DNS points at your server.

## Steps

1. Decide the hostname, for example book.yourcompany.com.
2. Add the DNS record at your DNS provider.
3. Set the domain in slotLane and wait for the certificate.
4. Open the domain and book a test slot.

## Common mistakes

- Testing too early. DNS can take a while. A failing certificate fetch usually just needs time.
