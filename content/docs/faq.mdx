<Faq q="Do guests need an account?">
No. Guests open your booking link, choose an available time and enter their details.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Which timezone will guests see?">
Available times are shown in the guest's timezone. You set your availability in your own timezone. See [Time zones](/docs/how-it-works/time-zones).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can guests reschedule or cancel?">
Yes. The confirmation email includes a link to manage the booking, with options to reschedule or cancel.
</Faq>

<Faq q="What does it cost?">
Try everything free for 30 days, no card needed. Then it’s free for one person, or $10 a month for each person on your team. Or $8 a month, billed yearly ($96 a person).
</Faq>

<Faq q="What happens after the 30-day trial?">
Nothing is deleted. You can stay on the free plan for one person, or subscribe at $10 a month for each person who can be booked ($8 billed yearly). Subscribing brings back your team, round robin, collective events, routing forms and paid bookings.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Who counts as a person?">
Anyone guests can book, you included. People you deactivate do not count, and you can change the number of seats at any time.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can my team use it?">
Yes. Add the people guests can book, each with their own hours, calendar and sign-in. Round robin shares bookings fairly across the team. Collective events need everyone free at once. See [Team scheduling](/docs/guides/team-scheduling).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I use my own domain?">
Yes. Guests book at book.yourcompany.com. You prove the domain is yours with one DNS record, and the page is served over HTTPS. See [Custom domain](/docs/guides/custom-domain).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Does it create the meeting link?">
A meeting type can be Google Meet, Zoom, a link or in person. Google Calendar bookings can include a Google Meet or Zoom link in the invite.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I move over from Calendly?">
Yes. Paste a Calendly access token and slotLane imports your event types and weekly hours. You pick what to bring over before anything is written.
</Faq>
